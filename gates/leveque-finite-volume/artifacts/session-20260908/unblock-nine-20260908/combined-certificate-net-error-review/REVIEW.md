Its result is specific to the pinned C-infinity overlay; it must not be described as a check against the unchanged ordinary production regularity.

Two applicability limits remain. The constructed array is a full integer line; it need not equal the extraction of a finite physical array with its fixed ghost values. The execution corollary therefore still correctly requires `hproj`, admission, compatible realization, and the threshold inequality. It does not construct such an execution. Also, its comparison target is the selected **one-dimensional** reference average; it is not yet an identified average of a supplied field on measured multidimensional cells. The rates are uniform within the fixed family, not asserted uniform across different families/directions. These are limits of the supplied corollary, not failures of its proof.

## What is still needed for a combined physical accuracy claim

The packets provide compatible foundations, but do not themselves prove a combined theorem. Such a claim still needs an actual shared family of physical references/projections and admissible executions; their equality with the one-dimensional comparison where that route is used; or, using the more general measured-capacity route, a uniform bound on the **net** physical flux defect with constants fixed before refinement/selected execution. The example does not supply that asymptotic bound. A joint nonvacuous physical consumer should accompany a claimed combined application. No need to replace the net bound with separate per-face orders was found.
