2. **`RiemannInformationCoordinateSweep.lean`**: place `SweepAdmitted`, transfer of admission between fallbacks, full-sweep fallback independence, admission at any executed prefix, actual selected face observation at that prefix, and the admitted two-stage mass identity. Do not replace it by an assumed total solver or inferred invariance.

3. **`CartesianGridGeometry.lean`**: place the needed geometry independently of any Riemann routine. The mathematical input can be the existing family `axis : D → OneDimensionalFiniteVolumeGrid`; no new nominal `TensorGrid` wrapper is needed. Define the product cell box, product width/volume, transverse face factor and transverse box as semantic functions of that family. Retain positive volume, width-times-area factorization, measured cell/transverse-volume identities, invariance under normal-index update, the actual face embedding/normal and transverse coordinates, and adjacency of physical face positions. These are direct compositions of the already canonical one-dimensional grid, `Function.update`/finite products and Mathlib `Real.volume_pi_Ico_toReal`. Any old-to-new comparison must explicitly apply the frozen `TensorGrid.axis`; changing representation must not be described as a nominal DefEq.

4. **`CartesianCoordinateUpdate.lean`**: place the area-weighted full-line numerical rule and its actual line-restriction equality with canonical `riemannFiniteVolumeUpdate`. Derive the admitted information specialization using the exact weighting/guard identity, line admission and same-result interface-flux identification, and retain measured Cartesian cell balance. The arbitrary full-line rule has no automatic accuracy conclusion. Use explicit coordinate-line lambdas or existing function-update identities, rather than promoting scratch `Cell`/`line` aliases merely to support a name. This leaf requires the first and third leaves. It is the necessary production Cartesian consumer; generic supplied-volume algebra alone does not replace it.

5. **`Examples/LeftStateCoordinateSweep.lean`**: retain a small positive-time two-direction nonvacuity example using the canonical ordered-pair method and existing unit-speed law. Give its concrete stripe array locally on `(Fin 2 → ℤ)` so no frozen Tensor namespace is imported. Reuse the selected flux and actual update theorems; do not duplicate a hyperbolicity proof or an interval-integral estimate. A constant direction/duration method-family alias can remain private. The example establishes finite execution, not general numerical accuracy.

The field-adapter and old exact Tensor comparison theorems in this packet are migration/compatibility evidence against frozen scratch objects. The old full-field coordinate API and exact total `LineSolver` are not public canonical APIs that must be duplicated or preserved under fresh public aliases. Keep those comparisons in placement evidence.
